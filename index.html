YOUR QUESTIONS, UNRAVELLED

Buying handmade crochet in Australia

Can I request a custom colour or size?

Yes, send your idea, colour preferences, dimensions, budget and preferred date. We review each request and confirm what is possible. Read our custom order guide.

How do I buy a crochet piece?

Add your favourites to the cart to request a quote, or enquire about one item. We confirm prices, availability and delivery before you pay. No card payment is taken here.

Can I enquire from anywhere in Australia?

Yes. Include your postcode so we can confirm delivery availability and cost. Adelaide collection may be discussed by arrangement. Read about ordering and delivery.

How long does a custom crochet order take?

Making time depends on the design, materials and current orders. Share your deadline when you enquire and we will confirm a timeframe before you commit.
